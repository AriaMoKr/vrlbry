The first release of vrlbry: a candle-lit 3D library for Kiwix ZIM files.

**Try it:** <https://ariamokr.github.io/vrlbry/>, with nothing to install. On a Meta Quest, open the
address in its browser and choose *Enter VR*.

**The library**
- Project Gutenberg, Wikipedia and Wikisource ZIM files become rooms of bookcases: real covers,
  authors and popularity for Gutenberg, a work per book for Wikisource, and numbered
  encyclopedia volumes of 1,000 articles for a Wikipedia.
- Walk the shelves, take a book out, open it and turn its pages: typeset pages with the books'
  own pictures, tables and formulas; text size, three page themes, a contents list.
- Links work: a book's contents and footnotes, Wikipedia's references, sections and other
  articles (the right volume comes off the shelf), with Back along the links you followed.
- Search finds any book by title or author, and any Wikipedia article by title or another name.
- Big libraries are browsed by genre and title letter, up to 10,000 books in a room, with pages.

**Where it runs**
- A VR headset (Meta Quest; WebXR), a desktop (mouse and keyboard, or an Xbox or PlayStation
  gamepad) and a phone (touch).
- Online: a demo set on the site, Kiwix's whole library to open from the web (read in place, a
  few kilobytes at a time), and ZIM files from your own device, all in the browser.
- Or run the Node.js server on a folder of ZIM files (`npm start`), with HTTPS for a headset on
  your network.

See the [README](https://github.com/AriaMoKr/vrlbry#readme) for the controls and how to run it.
