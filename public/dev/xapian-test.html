Xapian search (experimental)

Searches a ZIM's full-text index read over the web (public/js/core/zim/xapian.js; docs/SEARCH.md, option 4): ?zim=<address> (Kiwix's mirror), &zimproxy=<proxy> or off, &inflight=<n>, &title for the title index. No stemmer here: type the words as stems ("whale", "harpoon").
